Our Story

Crafted for the Modern Plant Parent

PotHub was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to create beautiful, functional plant containers that elevate both the plant and the space it lives in. We believe that the vessel matters as much as what grows inside it.

Every piece in our collection is designed in-house and produced in partnership with skilled artisans around the world. From Portuguese ceramics to Japanese concrete techniques, we source the best methods and materials.
