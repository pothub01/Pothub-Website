Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the US and Canada. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 10-14 business days.
Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $100. This applies automatically at checkout with no code needed.
We currently ship to the United States, Canada, UK, Australia, Germany, France, and select other countries. You can see available shipping destinations during checkout.

Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Simply initiate a return through your account dashboard or contact our support team.
Log into your account, go to Order History, and select the order you wish to return. Follow the prompts to generate a prepaid return label. Drop off at any authorized carrier location.

Product Care

Wipe ceramic pots with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid abrasive cleaners. For stubborn stains, a paste of baking soda and water works wonders. Always dry thoroughly.
Our outdoor planters are designed to withstand frost, but we recommend bringing delicate ceramic pieces indoors during extreme cold. Concrete and stone planters are fully frost-resistant.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number. You can also track your order in your account dashboard under Order History.

Payments

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are encrypted and secure.
